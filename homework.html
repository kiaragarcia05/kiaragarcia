Homework 3 and 4 — Social Campaign Photomontage

Social Campaign Photomontage — Final

Photoshop Layers Screenshot

Two-Paragraph Project Explanation

For my photomontage, I wanted to be able to express something related to fantasy. I was purely inspired by Studio Ghibli which mixes nature with old illustrations and fantasy. I decided to pick a green landscape and blue skye as the background . Later I would layer different illustrations of the sun, moon and an old city, on top of that I put two people in the middle of it to represent humans and nature balance with nature. In this case the landscape represents nature , the abstract face drawing represents the human mind and thoughts, lastly we have the sun and the moon that represents time and balance. This is meaningful for me to create because i deeply connect with the moon a lot and enjoy watching it, same thing with nature which we humans hardly seem to be able to protect. I also created a star pattern in illustrator and added it to the sky to make the photomontage look more alive. I wanted the different images to look like they belonged to the same imaginary world I was trying to create even though all of them come from different time lines. These images I was able to get from the resources provided, this was honestly such a hard job for me and in the photomontage itself you can see it’s not perfect but I really did enjoy cutting out some parts to make the pictures blend almost perfectly.

To create the photomontage i used photoshop and combined all of the images into one composition, then i used the layer mask to remove and hide parts of the images without deleting them completely especially around the illustrations. In order for me to do this I had to use a brush and set its percentage to how big I wanted the brush to be in order to get around the small corners. Of course it didn’t come out perfectly (i tried). I also used a blending mode (multiply) to make the middle illustration blend into the skye and landscape instead of having a solid background around it, at first the picture had a rectangle around it so again I had to erase most of it (not perfectly done). I changed the scale and placement of the different elements which in this case I picked the stars to make the photomontage less boring and more interesting. Once I finished on illustrators by duplicating the stars and then copying them and pasting them into photoshop as a (smart object) which allowed me to include a vector element while keeping its quality the way it was. This experience was definitely something hard for me since i never touched photoshop like that in my life but it was definitely fun to do this and being able to express my creativity side.
